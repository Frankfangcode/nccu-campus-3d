Law College to Education College walking survey (2026-10-12)
  43 additional user-provided photographs (059-102, excluding missing 097).
  Survey index and model placement: assets/data/walking_survey.json.
  Swimming Pool, dormitories, attached kindergarten, Li Yuan, Phoenix Grove,
  Jing Tang and connecting paths: scripts/law_education_route.gd.
  Relative arrangement uses the user-supplied NCCU downhill campus map.
  The map's planned-law-building label is superseded by the supplied photos.
  No GPS or measured dimensions were available; geometry is an interpretation.
  Original photos and local thumbnail galleries are not uploaded or packaged.
  Facade tile shader and geometry are procedural; foliage reuses the original
  project's generated leaf texture. Parked vehicles are unbranded primitives.
